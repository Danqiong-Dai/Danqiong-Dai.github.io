About

I am a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Physics and Astronomy at Western University. My research focuses on land surface modelling, regional climate modelling, and human–Earth system interactions.

A major focus of my research is understanding how human activities, particularly agricultural irrigation and groundwater pumping, influence the terrestrial water cycle. I am also interested in interactions between human management activities and climate extremes.

My research combines process-based land surface and regional climate models with observational datasets and computational approaches to investigate hydrological and climate processes.

Research Interests

Land Surface Modelling

Improving the representation of land-surface and hydrological processes in numerical models.

Regional Climate Modelling

Understanding regional climate processes and evaluating climate-model simulations.

Human–Earth System Modelling

Representing human activities and their interactions with terrestrial water and climate systems.

Groundwater & Irrigation

Investigating the impacts of groundwater pumping and agricultural irrigation on the terrestrial hydrological cycle.
